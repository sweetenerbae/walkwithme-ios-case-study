Walk With Me: Daily Walks

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This policy describes data processing in Walk With Me: Daily Walks. The developer and person responsible for processing is Diana Kuchaeva. For privacy requests and support, contact dianakuchaeva@hotmail.com.

1. Account and profile

We use your email, account identifier and authentication information for registration, sign-in and account recovery. Supabase Auth handles authentication. Your username, optional avatar, visibility settings and statistics support profiles and shared walks. Do not upload a photo you do not want other users to see.

2. Walks, motion and location

We store participants and invite recipients, walk mode and status, times, steps and each participant's distance. These records support invitations, progress synchronization, history and statistics.

With iOS permission, the app uses Motion & Fitness data to count steps and precise location to calculate distance. Coordinates are processed on the device; the current implementation does not save a GPS route in the walks database or display your exact position to a partner. Apple's geocoding service may process coordinates to determine a city and country. City and country code may be stored in random walk sessions subject to your sharing setting.

3. What other walkers see

In a regular walk, your partner sees your profile, steps and distance. Other users may see your username, avatar, statistics, walking rhythm, active-walk status and last-walk time, subject to visibility settings. Shared history includes information about both participants.

Random mode hides the partner's username and avatar in the interface. This is not anonymity from the server: sessions and events remain linked to account identifiers. The city-sharing setting controls city sharing in random walks. Changing a setting should not be treated as deletion of previously stored information or information another person has already seen.

4. Analytics and notifications

We record app opens, sign-ins, registrations, invitation creation, acceptance and decline, and walk starts and completions. Events may include a timestamp, user, session and other participant identifiers, and walk mode. This account-linked analytics helps us understand feature use and improve the product.

Push delivery may involve storing a device token, account identifier, platform and device locale. Apple Push Notification service handles delivery. The current version has no advertising SDKs, sale of personal data or cross-app advertising tracking.

5. Permissions and local storage

Camera and selected photo access are used to set an avatar. The device stores preferences, authentication data, cached avatars and history, and walk recovery information. Clearing cache, signing out and uninstalling the app are not equivalent to deleting your server account.

You can change location, motion, camera, photo and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Refusing access may limit the corresponding feature, such as step counting or photo selection.

6. Service providers and transfers

Supabase provides authentication, database, synchronization and avatar storage. The project's primary data is hosted in AWS region eu-west-1 (Ireland). Apple provides platform services, geocoding and notification delivery. If you email support, Microsoft/Outlook also processes your message and attachments. Never send passwords or sign-in codes to support.

Infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and protect the service. Processing may take place outside your country. Applicable contractual and legal safeguards depend on the provider and processing region. Access must be limited to service purposes and applicable data protection obligations. We may disclose necessary information where required by law or to protect users.

These document webpages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical visit logs. We do not add advertising trackers or our own analytics cookies to these pages.

7. Retention and deletion

Account information, history and account-linked analytics events are retained while the account exists unless you request deletion or another applicable retention basis exists. There is currently no automatic fixed-age expiry for analytics events.

The project uses the Supabase Free Plan. This plan does not include automatic project backups. We do not state a backup-retention period because the current provider configuration does not create scheduled backups. Necessary records may be retained for legal obligations, security or disputes. Local copies on other devices may remain until refresh or cache clearing.

Start account deletion from the app's settings or email dianakuchaeva@hotmail.com from your account address. We may need to verify ownership to protect your information. Deletion covers the account, profile, avatar, notification tokens, related walks and the user's analytics events. Deleting a shared walk may also remove it from the partner's history.

8. Your rights and processing grounds

Account features, synchronization and history are processed to provide the requested service. Device permissions and optional features operate with your permission. Analytics, security and support serve product improvement and reliability; where separate consent is required, the relevant legal requirements apply.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or portability, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to a competent authority. Email dianakuchaeva@hotmail.com; we will consider your request within applicable statutory periods. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

9. Children and security

The app is not specifically designed for children. Use must comply with local minimum-age and guardian-consent requirements. Contact us if you believe a child submitted data without required consent.

Protection uses authentication, access controls and secure connections. No system can guarantee absolute security. Do not share your password and consider what information you publish in your profile.

10. Updates and contact

We update this policy and its revision date when processing changes. We will communicate material changes through the app or another available channel and request consent when required. Developer contact: Diana Kuchaeva, dianakuchaeva@hotmail.com.
